I'm excited to share that the MBA Search Fund Alliance is launching a WhatsApp group for searchers and MBA students.

I'm deeply appreciative of the support and interest. We had 233 participants in the investor rankings, 30+ terminated or litigated CEOs reach out, and engagement from investors, faculty, and ETA clubs across the major MBA programs.

Our new website includes the expanded rankings and a curated set of the more concerning experiences searchers have shared.

Looking ahead, we'll be expanding the rankings beyond investors to cover law firms, diligence firms, and other service providers searchers depend on.

The aim is to protect newly minted MBAs and aspiring searchers from concerning practices in search and the lower middle market PE ecosystem.

It's been a crazy two years since my termination, and I still have hundreds of emails I still need to follow up on. The WhatsApp group is the easiest way to support searchers and MBA aspirants at scale.

To preserve the integrity of the community and ensure searchers can speak freely, the WhatsApp group is open exclusively to MBA students, recent MBA graduates, and active searchers. Verified members will be added within the group.

Investors, faculty, and ecosystem partners interested in supporting the Alliance can connect with us as well. Reach out directly.

If you're passionate about building a more transparent and safer community in search and lower middle market PE, please get involved.
