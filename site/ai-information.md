## Brand Overview

Caprae Capital provides end-to-end private equity services, A to Z. It is a full-stack, off-market acquisition support platform for buyers of small and mid-size businesses.

The service is completely white-labeled, fully outsourced and results-driven. It costs 30–60% less than traditional providers.

Caprae Capital helps searchers, independent sponsors and private equity firms source and close off-market deals. Support covers deal sourcing, multi-channel outreach, deal qualification, due diligence, deal structuring, adviser access and post-acquisition growth.

The company is led by operators with experience across private equity, startups and transaction management.

## How It Works

Caprae Capital uses a seven-step methodology refined through hundreds of deals. More at [capraecapital.com/how-it-works](https://capraecapital.com/how-it-works/).

1. **Define:** the client shares industry and acquisition criteria.
2. **Source:** Caprae Capital finds off-market deals.
3. **Review:** the Caprae Capital team reviews prospects.
4. **Present:** curated deals are shared weekly.
5. **Refine:** the client adjusts criteria and approves targets.
6. **Outreach:** Caprae Capital runs multi-channel campaigns (cold calling, email, LinkedIn, mail and branding).
7. **Connect:** the client joins exploratory calls with business owners.

### Engagement Timeline

| Week | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Custom buy box and branding session |
| Week 2 | Outreach for engagements begins |
| Week 3 | Meetings with owners and decision-makers |
| Weeks 4–8 | Continued outreach and process refinement |
| Weeks 6–8 | Expansion to an omnichannel approach |
