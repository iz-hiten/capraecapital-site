Common Questions

What is Caprae Capital?

Caprae Capital is a family office investment platform built for the lower middle market. We help searchers, independent sponsors, and PE firms source and close off-market deals, while also acquiring and building companies of our own. Led by experienced founders, advisors, and serial tech entrepreneurs, Caprae Capital invests across technology, finance, and media with a focus on building long-term, founder-focused businesses.

What is entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA)?

Entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA) means buying and running an existing business instead of starting one from scratch. Caprae Capital supports ETA buyers with deal sourcing, owner outreach, due diligence support, and post-acquisition growth.

Who does Caprae Capital work with?

We work with searchers and acquisition teams, including traditional and self-funded searchers, independent sponsors, entrepreneurs, private equity firms, and investment banks.

What does "white-labeled" private equity services mean?

Our team works under your name and brand. Business owners, partners, and investors deal with your firm, while Caprae Capital handles sourcing, outreach, and support in the background.

How does Caprae Capital find off-market businesses for sale?

We reach business owners directly through cold calling, email, LinkedIn, and handwritten letters, based on your acquisition criteria. Clients see 10–20 owner engagements per month, and we've helped close $110M+ in deals.

What is Search as a Service?

Search as a Service is Caprae Capital's proprietary deal sourcing program. We build and run a full off-market sourcing system around your acquisition criteria. It covers research, lead generation, prospecting, cold calling, email outreach, PR and branding, and due diligence support once a deal is under LOI, so you can spend your time talking to owners and closing deals, not building a sourcing team from scratch.

How is Search as a Service different from building an in-house sourcing team?

An in-house team means hiring callers and analysts and paying for data and software, and it takes months to get running. With Search as a Service, the team, clean data, and tracking tools are ready from day one, all for one monthly fee.

How do you find off-market businesses to buy?

We build target lists from your buy box, clean and validate the contact data, and reach owners directly through cold calling and email campaigns. These businesses aren't listed for sale, so you get to talk with owners before anyone else does.

Who is Search as a Service built for?

Search as a Service is built for any acquisition entrepreneur, including traditional and self-funded searchers, independent sponsors, private equity firms, and anyone sourcing off-market deals. Every campaign is set up around your own criteria for industry, size, and location.

What if I want more than 10 to 20 engagements a month?

We always recommend starting with the standard package. Once you're familiar with the process, you can ramp up to our Search as a Service Pro package for more outreach volume and more booked meetings a month.

Post-acquisition strategy is the plan for running and growing a business after you buy it. Our service helps owners scale acquired businesses through growth program design, fractional executive support, operational optimization, process automation, and performance metrics.

What should I focus on in the first 100 days after buying a business?

In the first 100 days, focus on keeping key staff and customers, learning how the business runs, and setting clear performance metrics before making big changes. We help you design a growth program and track the numbers that matter.

What are fractional CXO services?

Fractional CXO services give you senior leaders, such as a fractional COO, CFO, or CMO, on a part-time basis. You get executive experience without paying a full-time salary.

When does a business need a fractional COO?

A fractional COO makes sense when operations are holding back growth but a full-time COO isn't affordable yet. This is common right after an acquisition, when processes need fixing and the team is still settling in.

Do handwritten letters work for buying a business?

Yes. A handwritten letter stands out from the inbox and the phone. It works best as part of a full outreach campaign, reaching the owners who haven't responded yet to a call or email.

How do you write a letter to a business owner about buying their business?

Keep it short and personal. Say why you're interested in their business, briefly who you are, and that there's no pressure. End with one simple next step, such as a short call.

What is robotic handwritten mail?

Robotic handwritten mail uses machines that write with real pens, so each letter looks handwritten. It lets you send personal-looking letters at scale without writing each one by hand.

Does direct mail work for reaching business owners?

Yes, especially owners who don't answer cold calls or emails. We use personalized direct mail as one channel in a campaign that also includes calls, email, and LinkedIn.

Caprae Capital is led by operators who have bought, built, and scaled companies themselves. We cover everything from sourcing to post-close growth, work under your brand, and cost 30-60% less than traditional providers.

What is proprietary deal flow?

Proprietary deal flow means deals you find by contacting owners directly, not through brokers or auctions. You face less competition, can build a relationship with the owner, and often get better terms.

What's the difference between off-market and broker-listed deals?

Broker-listed deals are marketed to many buyers at once, which drives up competition and price. Off-market deals come from reaching owners who haven't listed their business, so you're often the only buyer in the conversation.

What is a buy box?

A buy box is your acquisition criteria: industry, revenue or EBITDA range, location, and the type of owner or business you want. We use your buy box to build every target list and outreach campaign.

Do you source deals in the lower middle market?

Yes. We focus on small and mid-sized businesses, including lower middle market companies, for searchers, independent sponsors, and PE firms.
