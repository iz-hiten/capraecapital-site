## 4. Search as a Service (flagship)
Page: https://capraecapital.com/services/search-as-a-service/

- Caprae Capital's outsourced, proprietary deal sourcing program. It builds target lists from your buy box, reaches business owners by phone, email, LinkedIn and handwritten mail, and passes you qualified owner conversations. You decide which deals to pursue; Caprae Capital runs the sourcing behind it, under your firm's name.
- Price: from $1,250 per month plus a 2% success fee. Clients typically see 10 to 20 owner engagements a month. Used by about 80% of clients.
- Proprietary deal sourcing means finding acquisitions by contacting owners directly, not through brokers or auctions; the result is proprietary deal flow, where you are often the only buyer at the table.
- Included (managed by Caprae Capital's team from week one): lead generation and prospecting (target lists built to your buy box and refreshed as the campaign learns); list cleansing and LeadGen QA software (removes bad and duplicate records); cold calling by callers trained on acquisition conversations; personalized email automation; owner-level LinkedIn outreach; handwritten letters for owners who ignore calls and email; cold call analytics (weekly reports on volume, connect rates and qualified conversations); deal advisory support (modeling, research and diligence setup once a deal is live); PR and branding; financial modeling; market research; due diligence setup; a team of associates.
- Process: 1) define your buy box (industry, size, geography, owner profile), agreed in week one; 2) build the target list (owner data matched to the buy box, cleaned and QA'd); 3) run multi-channel outreach (calls, email, LinkedIn and mail in parallel from weeks two to three); 4) hand off qualified owners with call notes and next steps.
- Who it's for: self-funded and traditional searchers, independent sponsors, PE firms and investment banks (white-labeled sourcing capacity for platforms and add-ons), and anyone sourcing off-market deals. Every campaign is set up around your own criteria for industry, size and location.
- Versus an in-house sourcing team: in-house means hiring callers and analysts and paying for data and software, and takes weeks to months to get running; Search as a Service has the team, clean data and tracking tools ready from day one, typically reaching first outreach within the first weeks (timelines are typical, not guaranteed), covers calls, email, LinkedIn and direct mail, and Caprae Capital handles the management overhead.
- More volume: start with the standard package; once familiar with the process, you can ramp up to the Search as a Service Pro package for more outreach volume and more booked meetings a month.
- Led by people who have closed acquisitions themselves; Hereford Johnson closed deals in 11 and 6 months as a buyer. After close, the same team can run your first 100 days through Post-Acquisition Strategy.

## 6. AI-Readiness
Page: https://capraecapital.com/services/ai-readiness/

- An AI readiness assessment that scores a business across six areas and ranks the AI use cases with the fastest payback, delivered with a 90-day roadmap. For small business owners, searchers evaluating a target, and PE portfolio companies. Custom pricing: a scoping call confirms the systems in scope, then a fixed quote is sent.
- Six areas scored: data quality and access (data gaps to fix first); systems and tool stack (tool-stack optimization plan); process documentation (automation candidates); team skills and adoption (training and adoption plan); security and compliance (guardrails before rollout); ROI and use-case fit (ranked use-case list).
- Process: discovery interviews → systems review → use-case scoring (value and effort) → 90-day roadmap with ROI estimates → readout with your team.
- Implementation: the same team can implement the roadmap: AI-driven automation (highest ROI first), tool stack optimization, and team training and adoption.
- AI due diligence for M&A: reviews a target's systems, data and AI exposure before you sign (outdated or unsupported software, key-person dependency on one IT contractor, data that can't be migrated, AI opportunities for the value creation plan). It complements, and does not replace, formal legal and financial diligence.
- Led by co-founder Zackary Beckham (10+ years of technology and AI implementation experience).

## 8. Post-Acquisition Strategy
Page: https://capraecapital.com/services/post-acquisition-strategy/

- Helps new owners run and grow a business after close: 100-day plan, value creation plan, fractional COO, CFO and CMO support, and coaching for first-time CEOs. Custom pricing based on the size of the business and the support needed.

## 11. How an engagement works
Page: https://capraecapital.com/how-it-works/ and https://capraecapital.com/services/

Seven-step methodology: 1) Define: you share industry criteria; 2) Source: Caprae Capital finds off-market deals; 3) Review: the team reviews prospects; 4) Present: curated deals weekly; 5) Refine: adjust criteria and approve; 6) Outreach: multi-channel campaigns; 7) Connect: you join exploratory calls with owners.

Engagement timeline: weeks 1-2 customize the buy box and branding session; week 2 outreach for engagements; week 3 meetings with owners and decision-makers; weeks 4-8 continued outreach and process refinement; weeks 6-8 expansion to an omnichannel approach.

Acquisition lifecycle: Define (buy box) → Source (calls, email, LinkedIn, mail) → Qualify (screening, research, models) → Close (your attorney, lender and QoE provider complete the transaction) → Grow (100-day plan and value creation plan).

Diligence boundaries. Caprae Capital prepares: preliminary financial review; NDR, churn and customer concentration analysis; market research and competitor mapping; financial modeling for the acquisition; lender and investor introductions. Stays with your licensed advisors: quality of earnings (QoE) report; legal due diligence and contracts; tax structuring and advice; final valuation and investment decision; loan underwriting.

## 14. Definitions used on the site
Page: https://capraecapital.com/faq/

- Entrepreneurship through acquisition (ETA): buying and running an existing business instead of starting one from scratch.
- Buy box: your acquisition criteria (industry, revenue or EBITDA range, location, type of owner or business). Used to build every target list and outreach campaign.
- Proprietary deal flow: deals found by contacting owners directly, not through brokers or auctions; less competition, a relationship with the owner, often better terms.
- Off-market vs broker-listed: broker-listed deals are marketed to many buyers at once; off-market deals come from owners who haven't listed, so you are often the only buyer.
- Fractional CXO: senior leaders (COO, CFO, CMO) on a part-time basis.
- Engagements: conversations with business owners and decision-makers generated through outreach.
- The team can act as an outsourced SDR (appointment setting through phone, email and LinkedIn).
