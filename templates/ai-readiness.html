Caprae Capital's AI readiness assessment scores a business across six areas, then ranks the AI use cases with the fastest payback. It is built for small business owners, searchers evaluating a target and private equity portfolio companies. Pricing is custom and set after a short scoping call.

The goal is practical: fewer manual hours and better decisions, not new tools for their own sake.

AI Due Diligence for M&A

Buying a business? Technology risk affects value. Caprae's AI due diligence for M&A reviews a target's systems, data and AI exposure before you sign, so you can price in upgrade costs and plan the first year after close.

It covers outdated or unsupported software, key-person dependency on one IT contractor, data that can't be migrated, and AI opportunities that could support your value creation plan. It complements, and does not replace, your formal legal and financial diligence.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is an AI readiness assessment?

An AI readiness assessment reviews a business's data, systems, processes and people to find where AI can save time and money, and what has to be fixed first. Caprae turns the results into a prioritized roadmap.

What does an AI readiness audit include?

Discovery interviews, a systems and data review, process mapping, use-case scoring and a readout with a 90-day roadmap, tool-stack recommendations and a training plan.

How long does an AI readiness assessment take?

Most assessments take two to four weeks, depending on company size and how many systems we review.

How much does AI readiness consulting cost?

Pricing is custom and set after a short scoping call, based on business size and the number of systems and teams involved.

Do you offer AI due diligence for M&A?

Yes. For buyers, we review a target's technology, data and AI exposure so you understand the risks and upgrade costs before you sign.

Is AI worth it for a small business?

Usually, when it starts with repetitive tasks and existing workflows. We rank use cases by return first, so you don't pay for tools your team won't use.
