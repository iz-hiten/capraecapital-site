FAQ

Common Questions

What does a buy-side M&A advisor do?

A buy-side M&A advisor helps a buyer find, evaluate and close an acquisition. Caprae Capital covers the support side: sourcing off-market targets, preparing diligence research and planning growth after close.

How much do Caprae Capital's services cost?

Search as a Service starts at $1,250 per month plus a success fee. Entrepreneurship as a Service is a fixed monthly plan. AI-Readiness and Post-Acquisition Strategy are custom priced, and Tech Development is priced per project. Current figures are on the pricing page.

What is the difference between deal sourcing and deal advisory?

Deal sourcing finds and contacts owners who match your buy box. M&A deal advisory supports what comes next: qualifying, research, modeling and diligence preparation. Caprae offers both.

Does Caprae replace my lawyer, accountant or QoE provider?

No. Caprae prepares research and analysis that makes diligence faster, but legal review, tax advice, audited financials and quality of earnings reports should come from your own licensed advisors.
