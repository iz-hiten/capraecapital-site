Search as a Service is Caprae Capital's outsourced deal sourcing program. We build target lists from your buy box, reach business owners by phone, email, LinkedIn and handwritten mail, and pass you qualified owner conversations. Plans start at $1,250 per month plus a success fee, and clients typically see 10 to 20 owner engagements a month.

You decide which deals to pursue. We run the sourcing behind it, under your firm's name.

Proprietary deal sourcing means finding acquisitions by contacting owners directly, not through brokers or auctions. The result is proprietary deal flow: conversations where you are often the only buyer at the table.

FAQ

Common Questions

What is Search as a Service?

Search as a Service is Caprae Capital's outsourced deal sourcing program. We build target lists from your buy box, reach owners through cold calling, email, LinkedIn and handwritten mail, and hand you qualified owner conversations, so you can spend your time on owners and deals.

How much does Search as a Service cost?

Search as a Service starts at $1,250 per month plus a success fee on closed deals. Final pricing depends on the channels and outreach volume your search needs.

How is Search as a Service different from building an in-house sourcing team?

In-house means hiring callers, buying data and software, and months of ramp time. Search as a Service has the team, data and tools ready from week one.

What is a buy box, and how do you use it?

A buy box is your acquisition criteria: industry, revenue or EBITDA range, geography and owner profile. Every target list and outreach script in your campaign is built from it.
