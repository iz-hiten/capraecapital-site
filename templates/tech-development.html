Caprae Capital's Tech Development team provides custom software development, from startup MVPs to full-stack SaaS platforms, CRMs and websites. Projects run in agile sprints with built-in QA and are priced per project after scoping.

Because Caprae also sources and grows acquired companies, we build with an owner's priorities in mind: speed to value, maintainable code and tools a small team can run.

Startup MVP Development and Websites

MVP development is about learning fast. We agree the one or two jobs the product must do, build only those, and launch to real users. Startup MVP development with Caprae includes a scope document, clickable design, a working build, QA and launch support.

For a startup website design or a website for a small business, the same process applies at a smaller scale: clear messaging, fast pages and analytics from day one.

FAQ

Common Questions

What tech development services do you offer?

Custom software development from MVPs to full-stack platforms, SaaS products, CRMs and client portals, internal tools, digital marketing tools and websites, with project management and QA included.

How long does MVP development take?

A focused MVP typically takes 6 to 12 weeks, depending on scope and integrations. We agree the scope and timeline before work starts.

How much does custom software development cost?

Tech Development is priced per project, based on scope and complexity. You receive a written estimate after the discovery call.

Who owns the code?

Ownership terms are set in your project agreement.

Do you build websites for search funds and PE firms?

Yes. We build websites for search funds, independent sponsors and private equity firms, often alongside Branding as a Service.

Can you build tools for search funds or PE firms?

Yes. As part of Caprae Capital's end-to-end services, we build deal-tracking tools, investor portals and digital marketing tools for acquisition teams and the businesses they buy.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Post-launch support and further development are available on a monthly or per-sprint basis.
