Integration and Value Creation

Post-Acquisition Integration Strategy

A good integration plan protects what you paid for. A value creation plan private equity investors trust shows how you will grow it.

Integration checklist

  • People: retention, roles, communication
  • Systems: accounting, CRM, IT access
  • Finance: banking, reporting, lender covenants
  • Customers: introductions, contracts, pricing
  • Brand: what changes and when

Value creation levers

  • Pricing and packaging
  • Sales capacity and pipeline
  • Cost and process efficiency
  • Technology and AI
  • Add-on acquisitions

FAQ

Common Questions

What is post-acquisition strategy?

Post-acquisition strategy is the plan for running and growing a business after you buy it. Caprae builds your 100-day plan and value creation plan, provides fractional executive support and coaches first-time CEOs.

What should I focus on in the first 100 days after buying a business?

Keep key staff and customers, learn how the business actually runs, and set clear performance metrics before making big changes. Quick wins come after you understand the numbers.

What is a value creation plan in private equity?

A value creation plan lists the specific levers that will grow a company's value after acquisition, such as pricing, sales capacity, cost and add-ons, with owners, targets and review dates for each.

When does a business need a fractional COO?

When operations are holding back growth but a full-time COO isn't affordable yet. This is common right after an acquisition, when processes need fixing and the team is settling in.

What is a buy and build strategy?

A buy and build strategy acquires a platform company and then adds smaller add-on acquisitions to grow faster than organic growth alone. Caprae can source add-ons and plan their integration.
